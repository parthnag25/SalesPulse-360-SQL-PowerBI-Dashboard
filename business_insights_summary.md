# SalesPulse 360: Business Insights Summary

## Executive Overview

The business generated $92.5M in total revenue and $23.5M in contribution profit across 30,000 orders. Revenue increased year over year, but contribution margin declined, indicating that the company is growing while facing profitability pressure.

## Profitability Drivers

Electronics generated the highest revenue among product categories, but it showed weaker margin performance compared with categories such as Apparel and Office Supplies. Discount analysis also showed that higher discount bands are associated with lower profitability, especially transactions with discounts above 15%.

## Customer and Sales Performance

Corporate customers generated the highest revenue, making them an important customer segment for the business. However, customer and sales performance should be evaluated using both revenue and profitability metrics. Sales reps showed strong revenue attainment overall, but profit attainment was lower, which suggests that revenue growth alone does not fully represent sales effectiveness.

## AI-Assisted Insights

Power BI Key Influencers identified Electronics as the strongest driver of Low Margin, making products in this category 8.08x more likely to fall into the Low Margin group. The 20%+ discount band was also identified as a key driver of Low Margin.

These AI-assisted findings support the SQL and dashboard analysis by confirming that profitability pressure is mainly connected to product mix, discounting, and return-related costs.

## Recommended Business Actions

1. Review Electronics pricing and cost structure to improve margins.
2. Reduce unnecessary discounts above 20%.
3. Review Corporate Sales discounting because the channel drives revenue but has weaker profitability.
4. Investigate Electronics return drivers, especially defective and compatibility-related returns.
5. Evaluate sales reps using profit attainment, not only revenue attainment.
